Fintech Security Authenticator

FINTECH · SECURITY UX · MOBILE

The cryptography was fine. The words on screen were not — 73% of people told us they didn't understand what we wanted from them, and 68% left angry feedback. That's not an edge case; that's the product.

Authenticator

Case study

Overview

Role UI/UX Designer — end-to-end
Platform Mobile (iOS & Android)
Scope User Research · IA · Visual Design
Tools Figma, Maze, Hotjar

Financial institutions depend on authenticator apps to bridge high-assurance security with everyday tasks. This project focused on the human layer: turning opaque permission and verification flows into language people could act on under stress — without diluting compliance requirements or engineering constraints.

The problem

Security that confused everyone

73%

of users couldn't understand the verification prompts

68%

left angry or confused feedback after authentication

Confusing language

Technical jargon in prompts obscured what action was required.

Time pressure failures

Countdown timers increased panic and errors instead of clarity.

Recovery dead ends

No clear retry path after failures left users stuck and distrustful.

Research

Understanding the users

8 User Interviews Qualitative sessions on mental models, fear points, and vocabulary.
Hotjar heatmaps 70% never scrolled past the first row — critical actions were invisible.
Support ticket analysis Categorized 200+ tickets to find recurring failure patterns.
